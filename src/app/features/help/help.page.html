Getting started

  1. 1

    Choose a festival

    Browse the festival catalogue, search by name or year, and use filters to find an event with published timetable data.

  2. 2

    Plan your sets

    Open a festival to explore its line-up, add the artists you want to see, mark must-see sets, and spot timetable clashes.

  3. 3

    Keep your route close

    Return to Home for your next festival, live-set reminders, packing list, budget, and the details you need while you are there.

Festival data

  • Catalogue festivals and timed line-ups come from Timetable.lol, with attribution shown wherever its data is used.
  • The catalogue is currently refreshed through a scheduled sync, checked, and bundled into the app so browsing remains available offline.
  • Use App settings to change the colour theme, appearance, or background image.

Custom festivals

Planning something that is not in the catalogue? You can add a custom festival manually from the festival planner.
