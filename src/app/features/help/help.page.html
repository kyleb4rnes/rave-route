YOUR FESTIVAL COMPANION

Make every festival day count.

Rave Route keeps your plans, line-up, and practical details together on your device.

Getting started

  1. 1

    Add a festival

    Create your own festival or choose one from the catalogue. Add dates, location, travel, and accommodation details.

  2. 2

    Build your route

    Add sets to your line-up, mark the ones you must see, and use the timetable to spot clashes.

  3. 3

    Keep plans close

    Return to Home for your next festival, live-set reminders, and the details you need while you are there.

Quick tips

  • Festival and line-up information is stored locally on this device.
  • Use App settings to change the colour theme, appearance, or background image.
  • Browse catalogue festivals using search and the filter button, then import the ones you want to plan.
